FAQ

Common questions

Is it really free to use? +
Yes — the OpenStreetMap source is completely free, no API key required. The pitch email generator is also free. You pay $19.95/month for your Footprint subscription, but there are no additional per-scan or per-lead costs ever.
Do I need a Google Maps API key? +
No. OpenStreetMap works out of the box worldwide. If you want richer data or better coverage in dense cities, you can add your own Google Places API key in Settings. It's optional — and Google gives $200/month in free API credits, which covers thousands of searches.
How do the pitch emails work? +
Expand any lead in your Leads tab and click "Generate pitch email". Footprint instantly creates a personalised cold outreach email based on that business's name, location, type, and exactly what they're missing online. Copy it, or click "Open in Mail" to send it directly. No extra cost, no API key — built into your subscription.
How accurate is the data? +
OpenStreetMap is excellent in most cities but is community-contributed, so some businesses may have gaps. Every lead includes a direct Google Maps link so you can verify before reaching out. Using the Google Places source gives more complete and up-to-date data, especially for phone numbers and business hours.
Can I use this on my phone? +
Yes — open Footprint in Safari or Chrome on your phone and tap "Add to Home Screen" for a full app experience. It works great for in-person prospecting — scan the street you're standing on and see which nearby businesses don't have websites or social media.
What happens to my leads if I cancel? +
Your data stays in read-only mode for 90 days and you can export it as CSV any time. After 90 days of inactivity, accounts and data are deleted permanently.
