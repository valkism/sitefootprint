FAQ

Questions & answers

Does Footprint really work without an API key? +
Yes. Footprint uses OpenStreetMap's Overpass API and Nominatim geocoding, both of which are completely free and open. No Google API key, no Yelp key, no third-party paid service required. Your subscription covers Footprint itself — scanning is always free.
How accurate is the data? +
OpenStreetMap is very well-maintained in most cities worldwide, but it's community-contributed data — so some businesses may be missing details like website or phone even if they have them in real life. We always show a Google Maps link inside every lead so you can verify before you reach out.
Is my card safe? How does billing work? +
100%. Billing is handled entirely by Stripe — one of the world's most trusted payment processors. We never see, store, or touch your card details. Stripe is fully PCI DSS compliant. You can manage or cancel your subscription from the Settings page at any time, and Stripe's Customer Portal handles everything securely.
What happens to my leads if I cancel? +
Your leads are stored in your account. If you cancel, your account goes into a read-only state — you can still export your data as CSV. After 90 days of inactivity, accounts and their data are permanently deleted.
Can I use this internationally? +
Yes. OpenStreetMap covers 180+ countries with excellent data quality. Just type any location — Footprint uses Nominatim to find the exact coordinates, then queries OSM for businesses in that area. Works in any language that OSM supports.
Do you sell my prospect data? +
Never. Your leads are stored in your own account, scoped to your user ID, and completely private. We have no commercial interest in your prospect lists and no mechanism to share them. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
